I'm Matee ur Rasool Meladi, a final-year Electronics Engineering student at the Islamia University of Bahawalpur, driven to advance intelligent electronic systems and contribute to next-generation AI-powered hardware solutions.

My work spans embedded systems, FPGA design, and signal processing — turning raw sensor and channel data into working, real-time systems. I'm especially interested in modern wireless communication, including adaptive beamforming, and in radar, UAV, and defense-grade electronics.
